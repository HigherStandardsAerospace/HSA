Higher Standards Aerospace

About

Higher Standards Aerospace (HSA) is a leading support facility that specializes in repairing and overhauling aircraft fuel components. Established in 2017 by Frank Villasante, who acquired, relocated, and expanded the business, HSA has become a dynamic and innovative player in the aviation industry. Frank Villasante, former owner of High Standard Aviation, brings his pursuit of perfection, passion, and unsurpassed reliability to HSA. The team at HSA is comprised of dedicated professionals who are committed to providing their customers with the highest standards of quality, service, and customer support. The company’s focus on quality workmanship ensures safe and reliable operation of the components returned to service. HSA works directly with leading airline customers and is recognized around the world for its quality workmanship and component reliability. The highly specialized and experienced technicians continuously undergo education and training programs to stay up to date with the latest developments in the field. Additionally, HSA’s quality department has developed a sophisticated tracking program that provides data and feedback on the reliability of every part number. All work is strictly performed in accordance with the manufacturer’s manuals and specifications and/or FAA approved data. HSA operates in a 25,000 sq ft air-conditioned, state-of-the-art facility that is specifically designed to provide the highest quality and reliability to their customers.
