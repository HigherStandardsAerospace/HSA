PRIVACY POLICY

Contact form information

When you use the contact form, Higher Standards Aerospace receives the name, email address, and message that you provide. This information is used only to route and respond to your inquiry.

The form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. According to FormSubmit's published documentation, form submissions are retained by that service for up to 30 days. Higher Standards Aerospace receives the submitted information by email.

Please do not use the contact form to submit payment information, passwords, export-controlled information, or other sensitive technical data.

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information submitted through this form, contact sales@hsaero.com.
